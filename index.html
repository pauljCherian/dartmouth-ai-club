Who We Are

Our Community

A new way for Dartmouth students to engage with AI–particularly what's not covered in classes. Meet the people on campus who are actually building and researching AI. No prior experience required, just curiosity.

Member-Led Sessions

Each meeting, officers present on an AI topic of their choice–whether it's a research concept, recent paper, AI company deep-dive, or their own work. Topics range from foundational ML to cutting-edge research on agents, LLMs, and more.

Guest Speakers

We bring in speakers from across the AI landscape – research, industry, and academia – to share their expertise with our members.

"We're building a durable, student-led institution around current AI research, infrastructure, and applications–bringing together the substantial interest on campus that currently has no home."
